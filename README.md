# Family Meals: AI Meal Planner

An Android app for planning a family's meals and keeping the grocery list in step with them. It plans the week's breakfasts, lunches and dinners (by hand or with AI), keeps recipes with their ingredients, and lets you share the shopping list with anyone.

- **Application ID:** `org.coughlin.familymeals.planner`
- **Requires:** Android 8.0 (API 26) or later, targets Android 15 (API 35)
- **Language:** Java, MVVM with Room and LiveData

## Features

### Grocery list
- Shows every product you've put on the list.
- **Tap** an item to check it off as you buy it.
- **Swipe right** to remove an item and log it to your purchase history.
- **Search** from the toolbar to filter by name.
- **Clear** the whole list from the overflow (⋮) menu.

### Share the shopping list
Tap the **share icon** in the Grocery List toolbar to send the list to someone. It works like sharing a photo from the camera: the Android share sheet opens, and you pick Messages, Gmail, WhatsApp, Quick Share, a notes app, or any other app.

- Only items you **haven't checked off** are included, so the list shows what's left to buy.
- Email apps get the title as the subject line.
- If everything is checked off, the app tells you there's nothing left to buy instead of opening the share sheet.

The shared message looks like this:

```
Shopping List – Mon, Nov 23

☐ Bread
☐ Eggs
☐ Milk

Sent from Family Meals
```

### Products and history
- **Add Product:** type a name; matching products appear as suggestions after two characters. Long-press a suggestion to delete a duplicate.
- **History:** every item swiped off the grocery list is logged with its date.

### Weekly menu
- Swipe between the days of the week; each day has breakfast, lunch and dinner.
- **Generate Menu** has the AI fill empty meal slots for the coming week, and **Regenerate This Week** replaces the generated meals. Meals you set by hand are never overwritten.
- Generation can also run automatically on a schedule you choose in Settings.
- Dinners show a photo (needs a Pexels API key in Settings).
- Search past meals to reuse them.

### Recipes
- Each meal can have a recipe with ingredients, steps and an image, written by hand or generated by AI.
- **Get New Recipe** regenerates a recipe and updates the grocery list: new ingredients are added, and old ones no other recipe needs are removed.

### Google Calendar
- **Add to Google Calendar** on a meal opens a prefilled event.
- **Add Week to Google Calendar** writes the visible week to a calendar you choose; sharing again replaces the app's earlier events.
- Generated menus can be added automatically (toggle in the menu screen's overflow menu).

### AI providers
Choose a provider in **Settings**: Ollama (on your local network), Gemini, Claude, Groq, OpenAI, Grok, or an on-device model where the phone supports it. Model lists load live from each provider with prices shown. Prompts can be edited, and dictated with the microphone button.

The in-app **Help** screen (navigation drawer → Help) explains each of these.

## Building

Open `org.coughlin.android.fmm.v2.application1/` in Android Studio, or use Gradle from that directory:

```bash
cd org.coughlin.android.fmm.v2.application1
./gradlew assembleDebug      # debug APK
./gradlew bundleRelease      # signed release bundle (.aab) for Google Play
```

Uses the JDK bundled with Android Studio and the Android SDK (path in `local.properties`).

## Releasing to Google Play

Release builds are signed with the upload key named in `key.properties` (next to `settings.gradle`):

```properties
storeFile=/path/to/upload-keystore.jks
storePassword=...
keyAlias=...
keyPassword=...
```

`key.properties` and `*.jks` are git-ignored; keep backups of both off this machine. Google Play App Signing holds the app signing key. Increase `versionCode` in `orgcoughlinandroidfmmv2application/build.gradle` for every upload. The bundle is written to `orgcoughlinandroidfmmv2application/build/outputs/bundle/release/`.
